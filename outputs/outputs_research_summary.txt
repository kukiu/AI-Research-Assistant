Agentic AI is an advanced form of artificial intelligence that operates with limited supervision, enabling autonomous decision-making and action. Unlike traditional AI, which relies on commands or data analysis, agentic AI can set goals, plan, and execute tasks with minimal human intervention. It mimics human decision-making to solve problems in real time and can function autonomously in unstructured environments. This technology allows enterprises to expand automation beyond predefined tasks, tackling complex, end-to-end processes. Agentic AI is distinguished by its ability to operate independently, setting it apart from traditional software and AI that require constant prompting or guidance. It represents a significant advancement in AI capabilities, offering proactive and autonomous solutions for various applications.
